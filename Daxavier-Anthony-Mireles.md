## Verification-First Troubleshooting

A reliable troubleshooting process begins by defining the operating condition, verifying source voltage and phase configuration, checking the power path and control path separately, taking measurements at logical checkpoints, identifying the root cause, and verifying the correction under the intended operating condition.
